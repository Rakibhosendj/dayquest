AUTOMATIC UNIQUE PURCHASE BILL NUMBERS (2026-11-12)
- New purchase bills automatically receive a bill number based on their purchase date in MM-YYYY-N format (example: 11-2026-1).
- The sequence starts at 1 for each month and advances after the highest existing sequence for that month. Existing bill numbers are checked before a number is generated, so a new number is never reused or duplicated.
- The bill number is shown as a read-only preview and is recalculated when the purchase date changes. Editing a bill on its original date preserves its existing number; changing its date assigns a unique number for the new month.
- Older bills with no bill number are assigned a unique number during migration. Existing non-duplicate manual bill numbers are preserved; duplicate historical numbers are repaired.
- Backup imports also normalize missing or duplicate bill numbers. Existing purchase records and their inventory/cost assignments are preserved.
- Updated the app asset URL and service-worker cache version.


PURCHASE LINE AMOUNT ENTRY & AUTO-CALCULATION (2026-11-12)
- Made Line amount an editable field on each purchase bill product line.
- Added an explicit “When line amount is entered, calculate automatically” choice: calculate Rate per unit, or calculate Quantity.
- When calculating rate, DayQuest uses line amount ÷ quantity. When calculating quantity, it uses line amount ÷ rate. The required divisor is validated and a helpful inline message is shown if it is missing.
- Manually changing quantity or rate continues to recalculate the line amount. Line amounts are rounded to two decimal places for bill totals, while automatically derived quantity/rate values retain six decimal places.
- Saved bill totals, inventory cost, supplier balance, and member/family costing use the entered line amount, keeping the saved bill value consistent. Existing saved bill lines retain their recorded amounts and remain editable.
- Updated application asset URLs and service-worker cache version so deployed browsers load this change.


UPDATE — MULTI-MEMBER EXPENSES & GUEST STAYS

Expense assignment now uses checkboxes instead of a Family / single-member selector. Select one or more family members or registered guest names; the entry amount is divided equally among the selected people. Leave all people unselected to keep the expense unassigned.

Inventory product lots use the same multiple-person allocation. This makes purchase-product costs consistent with expense costs. Existing Family-assigned and individual-member expenses / inventory lines are migrated to member lists so historical data remains included.

Reports now show member-wise costs based on each entry's selected recipients. Guest names appear in the member-cost list while their stay overlaps the selected reporting period.

A Guest Entry & Stay Register is available in Reports. Add guest names, select multiple guests, enter the guest entry date and purpose, then save the stay. When guests leave, set the out date in the Guest Stays list. The Guest Cost Report shows entry date, out date, purpose, guest-purpose cost (single-guest assignments), total shared family cost during the stay date range, and the selected guests' allocated share of those shared costs.

Guest names, stays, assignments, and out dates are included in normal local storage and JSON backup export/import. The service-worker cache version was updated.

PURCHASE COST ADJUSTMENTS, CREDITOR DISCOUNTS & PRODUCTION UPDATE (2026-11-14)
- Purchase bills now include Transit Cost and Purchase Discount fields. The final bill total is products subtotal + transit cost - discount. Transit and discount are allocated proportionally across purchased product lines so each inventory lot receives a landed cost; inventory valuation, product costs, purchase reports, and member-cost reports use those allocated line costs.
- Supplier / creditor payments now accept a Discount Received amount. The cash/bank payment remains the actual amount paid, while the discount is applied as an additional settlement against the supplier's outstanding balance. Supplier account history and the outstanding creditor report show the discount separately.
- Added a Production menu and production register. A production batch can use one or more existing Inventory products and enter the quantity consumed from each material lot; materials are consumed FIFO and stock availability is validated against the selected production date.
- Production forms record the finished product name, product type, size/specification, output quantity and unit, plus electricity and transit costs. The app shows material cost, total production cost, and cost per output unit before saving.
- Saving production records the consumed inventory quantities and creates a finished-goods inventory lot with the calculated total and unit cost. Produced stock can be assigned to members, used in later production, or selected for Sales Memos. Production overhead is tracked separately in member/guest cost reports so ingredient purchase cost is not counted twice.
- Production batches and their inventory consumption records are included in backup export/import. A batch cannot be deleted after its finished output has been used or sold; deleting an unused batch restores its material quantities.
- Updated the application asset versions and service-worker cache name to refresh deployed files.
