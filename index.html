Control whether saved purchase bills can be edited from Purchase and Inventory.

When enabled, saved bill changes refresh inventory values and all reports. Bills with recorded stock usage, production or sales are locked. If the supplier has payment history, keep the supplier, purchase date and outstanding credit amount unchanged to protect creditor allocations. Equivalent units such as kg/g and litre/ml convert automatically while preserving the line amount.
